Bear Drive Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Bear Drive is a personal Android app for browsing your own Google Drive. It is not published on the Google Play Store and is used only by its developer and a few people the developer knows. This policy describes the current version of the app and will be updated before the app handles your information differently.

Information Bear Drive uses

When you sign in with Google and allow access to Google Drive, Bear Drive uses:

  • your Google account's email address and name, to show which account is signed in;
  • your Google Drive account identifier, to keep each account's information separate on the phone;
  • information about the files and folders inside the folders you open in the app: names, types, sizes, dates, owners' names, folder locations, photo and video dimensions, video length, and what your access allows you to do with each item.

Bear Drive requests full Google Drive access because it is built to browse, and in later versions manage, existing files anywhere in your Drive and in Shared with me. The current version only reads file and folder information. It does not download, change, upload, or delete files.

Where your information is kept

  • File and folder information and your account's email address and name are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, so folders you opened can be shown again quickly and while you are offline.
  • This information is excluded from Android backups and from transfers to a new phone.
  • The access Google grants to the app is kept only in memory while the app runs and is never written to storage.
  • Bear Drive has no server of its own. Information travels only between your phone and Google.

Sharing

Bear Drive does not sell, share, or transfer your information to anyone. It contains no advertising and no analytics, and it does not use your information for any purpose other than showing you your own Drive.

Bear Drive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your information

  • Sign out in Bear Drive's Settings removes the account's stored file and folder information, its email address and name, and its last opened folder from your phone. Per-folder display choices, such as list or gallery view, stay on the phone under one-way scrambled identifiers until the app is uninstalled.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
  • To withdraw Bear Drive's access to your Google account, remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections.
