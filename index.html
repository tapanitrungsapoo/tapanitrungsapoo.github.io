Bear Drive

A personal Android app for Google Drive

Bear Drive is an Android app for browsing the photos, RAW files, videos, and folders in your own Google Drive, including files other people have shared with you.

It is a private, personal project. It is not available on the Google Play Store and is used only by its developer and a few people the developer knows.

Bear Drive talks only to Google. It has no server of its own, no advertising, and no analytics.
